You go first. On your first turn, place both tokens anywhere on the 1–9 row below (they can both go on the same number) — that's your product, so mark it on the grid above. From then on, each turn you move one token to a new number and mark the new product. If that product is already claimed, you don't get to mark it — your turn just ends, no penalty beyond that. There's no dice involved — every move is your own choice. Whoever connects four in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) first wins — that's a BEELINE! The bot always multiplies correctly, but how far ahead it plans changes with difficulty.
