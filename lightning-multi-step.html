Problem shape
Ten two-step problems, asked one at a time — three 3-digit numbers joined by two operators, worked left to right. Type the total and press Check. A wrong answer tells you the right one and moves on; nothing is retried mid-round, because the point is to see which problems are quick and which aren't. At the end you get your score and a list of any you missed. Running totals never go below zero. This is a drill, not a strategy game — it exists so every fact can be asked directly, which the board games deliberately can't do.
