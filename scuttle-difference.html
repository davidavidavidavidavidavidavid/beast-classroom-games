Each round, roll the dice and split the digits into two numbers — you choose which digits go where, and in what order. (No number can start with a 0 unless every digit you rolled is a 0.) Subtract the smaller number from the larger — that's your score for the round. After three rounds, add your three round-scores together. Whoever has the smallest total that's still over the minimum wins. If nobody clears the minimum, the biggest total wins instead. Exact ties are friendly — you both win! The bot always calculates correctly, but how well it splits and arranges its digits changes with difficulty.
