The total starts at 0. Taking turns, each player adds 1 or 2 to the total — your choice, no dice involved. The instant the total reaches 10 or more, whoever just moved wins outright, even if their move pushed it past 10. Ties are impossible — the total only ever goes up, so someone always eventually crosses the line. Who goes first alternates every game, since going first is a real, provable advantage in this exact race. The bot always adds correctly, but how far ahead it plans changes with difficulty.
