The die is rolled one digit at a time — everyone sees the same digit at once. Each time a digit comes up, write it into one of your own remaining blanks: an inside blank (it counts toward your number) or a throw-away (it doesn't). Once written, a digit can never be moved or erased. Keep going until every blank is full. Whoever's inside number lands closest to the target without going over wins — going over pops your balloon and you lose outright, no matter how close you were. Ties are friendly! A rolled 0 can't fill the leading blank of your number while a throw-away or another blank is still open — there's nowhere better for it once everything else is full. The bot always adds correctly, but how well it decides inside-vs-throwaway changes with difficulty.
