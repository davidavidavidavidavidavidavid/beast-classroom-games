The pile starts at 20 tokens. Taking turns, each player removes 1, 2, or 3 tokens — your choice, no dice involved (you can't remove more tokens than are left). Whoever removes the last token wins. At the end of your turn, say how many tokens are left out loud. For this starting pile and these move amounts, going second is actually the stronger seat under perfect play — so who goes first alternates every round to keep things fair either way. The bot always subtracts correctly, but how far ahead it plans changes with difficulty.
