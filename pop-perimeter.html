Target perimeter
The die is rolled one digit at a time — everyone sees the same digit at once. Each time a digit comes up, write it into one of your own remaining blanks: your rectangle's length, its width (each counts twice toward the perimeter — that's what makes it a rectangle!), or a throw-away that doesn't count at all. Once written, a digit can never be moved or erased. Keep going until every blank is full. Whoever's perimeter (2 × length + 2 × width) lands closest to the target without going over wins — going over pops your balloon and you lose outright, no matter how close you were. Ties are friendly! A rolled 0 can't fill the leading blank of a side length while a throw-away or another blank is still open. The bot always adds correctly, but how well it decides where to place each digit changes with difficulty.
