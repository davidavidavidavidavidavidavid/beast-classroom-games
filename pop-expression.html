The die is rolled one digit at a time — everyone sees the same digit at once. Each time a digit comes up, write it into one of your own remaining blanks: an inside blank (it counts toward your number) or a throw-away (it doesn't). Once written, a digit can never be moved or erased. Keep going until every blank is full. Your inside number comes from combining the blank groups left to right using the + and − signs shown — that's what's compared to the target. Whoever's expression lands closest to the target without going over wins — going over pops your balloon and you lose outright, no matter how close you were. Subtracting more than you have left pops your balloon too — a negative result is an automatic loss, not just a bad score. Ties are friendly! A rolled 0 can't fill the leading blank of any inside group while a throw-away or another blank is still open — there's nowhere better for it once everything else is full. The bot always computes correctly, but how well it decides inside-vs-throwaway changes with difficulty.
