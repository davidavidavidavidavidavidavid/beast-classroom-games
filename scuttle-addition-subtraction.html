Each round, roll three digits and arrange them into a 3-digit number — you choose which digit goes in the hundreds, tens, and ones place. (The hundreds place can't be a 0, unless every digit you rolled is a 0.) After three rounds you'll have three numbers. Put them in whatever order you like, then choose to add or subtract the second and third from the first, and type in the total. Whoever's total lands closest to the target wins. Exact ties are friendly — you both win! The bot always calculates correctly, but how well it arranges its digits changes with difficulty.
