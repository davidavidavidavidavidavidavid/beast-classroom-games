The pile starts at 0¢. Taking turns, each player adds a nickel (5¢) or a dime (10¢) — your choice, no dice involved. Whoever's move brings the pile to exactly 50¢ wins. At the end of your turn, say the total value of the pile out loud. A dime is only offered when it wouldn't pass 50¢ — there's no reason to ever add a coin that overshoots the target on purpose, so this game doesn't offer that choice. Who goes first alternates every game, since going first is a real, provable advantage in this exact race — same as base Nim, just with coins instead of bare numbers. The bot always adds correctly, but how far ahead it plans changes with difficulty.
